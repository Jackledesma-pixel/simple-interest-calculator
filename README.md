# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator that calculates simple interest given the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

## Formula

Simple Interest = P × R × T

Where:
- P = principal amount
- R = annual rate of interest
- T = time period in years

## Input

The calculator takes three values:
- Principal amount
- Rate of interest
- Time period

## Output

The calculator displays the calculated simple interest.
